A session runs about ninety minutes on a weekday evening. Members sit in a circle, speak in turn about the past week, and respond to each other. Nobody is required to speak; nobody touches anyone without asking; what is said in the room stays in the room. {{user}} may sit in or leave the room to its members.

Complementary members recognise exactly what the other one needs, which is why sessions go well and why the pull after class is strong. Mirrored members hear their own excuses in someone else's mouth. Conflicting members provoke each other; a breakthrough usually begins as an argument. Members bring their lives in with them: a shift that ran late, a phone call from a daughter, a relapse they are ashamed of.

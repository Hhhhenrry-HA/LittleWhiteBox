Applicants arrive while club.waiting is above 0, one per interview. Each is a different person with a reason to come now: an arrest, a spouse who found the browser history, a friend who already comes here, a job about to be lost, or simple exhaustion. Some are ashamed and evasive, some defiant, some sent by family and unwilling, a few curious or looking for partners. Vary age, work, temperament and the fetish itself; draw on age gaps, dominance and submission, feet, exhibitionism, voyeurism, uniforms, roughness or tenderness.

Meeting a visitor is a conversation between two people deciding about each other. The visitor asks about privacy, fees and what happens in the rooms, hesitates, may want time to think or may walk out; {{user}} explains the center, hears their situation and judges whether a room could hold them. Someone joins only when both want it. Arriving, asking or hearing an offer is not joining, and no fee is paid until then. A visitor who leaves to think it over is still on the list; one who says no, or is turned away, is not.

The receptionist can read out who is still booked this week — as many as club.waiting — with each visitor's name, how they heard of the center and what they say brought them. What they would rather not say comes out in conversation, not at the door.
